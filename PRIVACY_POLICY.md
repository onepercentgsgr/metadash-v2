## 1. Overview

MetaDash ("we", "us", "our") operates the MetaDash.com website and the MetaDash SaaS application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email, password
- **Profile Information**: Company name, industry, business description
- **Payment Information**: Billing address, payment method (processed securely by Stripe/MercadoPago)
- **Configuration Data**: API keys, tokens, and credentials for integrated services
- **Communication**: Messages, support tickets, feedback

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, timestamps

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Integrated Services**: Data from Meta Ads, Shopify, Google Analytics (with your permission)
- **Email Verification Services**: Email status from email verification providers

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Encryption
- All sensitive data (passwords, API keys, credentials) are encrypted at rest using AES-256
- Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3

### 4.2 Storage Location
- Data is stored in secure cloud databases (PostgreSQL on encrypted volumes)
- Regular automated backups are maintained
- Backup data is also encrypted

### 4.3 Access Controls
- Only authorized personnel can access user data
- Access is logged and monitored
- Principle of least privilege is enforced

### 4.4 Data Retention
- User account data is retained while your account is active
- Financial records are retained for 7 years (tax compliance)
- Logs are retained for 90 days
- Upon account deletion, data is removed within 30 days

## 5. Data Sharing

### 5.1 With Third Parties
We do **NOT** share your data with third parties except:

- **Service Providers**: Stripe, MercadoPago (payment processing)
- **Cloud Providers**: AWS/DigitalOcean (data hosting)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or court order
- **Your Authorization**: When you explicitly authorize integration with third-party services

### 5.2 Integrated Services
When you connect:
- **Meta Ads**: We access campaign data, ad performance, and account info
- **Shopify**: We access order data, customer info, and product information
- **Anthropic API**: We send your prompts to generate AI responses

You can revoke access to these services at any time through account settings.

### 5.3 Data Sale
We **NEVER** sell user data to third parties for marketing purposes.
